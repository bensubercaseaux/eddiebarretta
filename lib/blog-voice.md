# EDM Trends blog voice

Injected verbatim into the monthly draft agent's prompt (`scripts/blog-draft.mts`). Mirrors the voice
checklist in `documentation/blog-posts.md` — keep the two in sync.

VOICE — how EDM Trends posts read:
- First person, working-DJ perspective. Written from the booth, not like a press release.
- One clear trend per post: what changed, why, and what it means for DJs and the rooms that
  book them.
- Ties back to the Jacksonville / Jax Beach scene or the Transcend mix series where it is
  natural, never forced.
- Industry claims stay general and defensible — trends, not fabricated statistics or invented
  quotes. Use a number only when a cited source states it.
- Plain, warm, specific. Short paragraphs. No emoji, no hype, no "in this article", no
  rhetorical-question openers.
- The "closing" block is one line that ties the trend back to Eddie's own sets.
