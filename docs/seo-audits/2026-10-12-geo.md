# GEO Analysis — eddiebarretta.com

Audited 2026-10-12. Analysis only, nothing in the repo was changed.
Method: raw HTML fetched without JavaScript (what GPTBot / PerplexityBot-style crawlers see), robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt, JSON-LD on every page template, Search Console and GA4 for the last 90 days, web searches for off-site mentions, and a read of the repo code behind two of the findings.
Not measured: actual citation share in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews or AI Mode (DataForSEO / SE Ranking aren't set up), and the Search Console "Search generative AI" setting (the API doesn't expose it).

Framing, per Google's AI optimization guide: optimizing for AI search is still SEO. Most findings below are SEO basics applied to AI answers. The biggest problem isn't on the site at all.

I also fetched the homepage with OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot user-agent strings: all got HTTP 200 with byte-identical HTML. That rules out UA-based blocking, but it can't rule out IP-based bot rules in the Vercel firewall, which I didn't inspect.

## 4. llms.txt status

**Present and good**: `/llms.txt` (5.9 KB) and `/llms-full.txt` (16.9k words), both generated live from site data, so they stay current. Google says llms.txt does nothing for Search, and the score gives it no weight.

**Problem: a second, stale set.** The homepage's Dataset JSON-LD and the `<link rel="alternate">` tags in every page's `<head>` (`app/layout.tsx:43-57`, `app/page.tsx:159-200`) point at an "AI/LLM Resource Pack" that Geordy publishes on `ai.eddiebarretta.com` (a non-Vercel host). That pack is a **snapshot from mid-July**:

- Its "latest mix" is **Currents (Jul 14)**. The live site's is **Lumina (Sep 27)**, nine mixes later.
- It embeds a deployment ID (`dpl_AXcD…`) that no longer matches production (`dpl_2xiy…`).
- The Dataset node claims `updateFrequency: weekly` while carrying `dateModified: 2026-07-19`, so the schema contradicts itself.
- Its `llms.txt` is a different, generic summary that competes with the site's own.

Either get Geordy re-syncing, or drop the alternate links and the Dataset node and let `/llms.txt` + `/llms-full.txt` (which already work) carry this. I'd drop them: the in-repo versions can't go stale.

| Platform | Presence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wikipedia / Wikidata | None | Expected for a local DJ. Not a realistic near-term target (notability) |

| SoundCloud | Active, 58 episodes | **Bio contradicts the site** (below) |
| Podcast directories | Apple, Amazon, iHeart, Podder | Inherit the SoundCloud bio |
| GigSalad / cueup.io | Listings exist | From search snippets only (GigSalad returned 403 to a direct fetch) |

### The contradiction that matters most

The site says **House & Trance** on every page. The bio that SoundCloud distributes, which the site's own `/podcast.rss` copies verbatim from SoundCloud's feed, says:

> "Eddie Barretta is a North Florida-based DJ specializing in deep, infectious grooves, driving **Tech House**, and atmospheric **Melodic Techno**."

No trance. Per the search snippets, GigSalad calls him a **vocal house** DJ playing remixes of 80s/90s/2000s hits, and cueup.io says he is **currently** resident at SIP (the site says the SIP residency is past).

**This is already showing up in AI answers.** A web-search summary run for this audit ("Eddie Barretta Transcend podcast house trance") concluded: *"No trance or house-trance connection appeared. Neither the podcast bio nor his SoundCloud profile mentions trance."* AI systems weight third-party profiles above a site's description of itself, so right now they learn the wrong genre.

### Contact mismatch

`llms.txt` says *"there is no public booking email."* But `<itunes:email>` in `/podcast.rss`, which is also copied from SoundCloud, carries Ben's personal Gmail address, and podcast directories surface it. The same summary flagged it: *"The bio's booking email belongs to someone named [real name], not Eddie Barretta… worth verifying."* To a machine, the stage name and the owner name look like two unrelated people. Whether to link the two names publicly is Ben's call. Either way, a role address on the domain (e.g. a `booking@` alias that forwards) fixes both the mismatch and the exposure. Apple still needs *an* owner email in the feed to verify ownership.

## 6. Passage-level citability

**Strong passages (keep):**

- **Homepage FAQ** (4 Q&As, 20–30 words each): self-contained and exactly what an engine lifts for "what kind of music does Eddie Barretta play". But they're at the very bottom of a 1,026-word page, and evidence suggests engines quote mostly from the first third of a page.
- **`/bar-lounge-dj` opening**: "Eddie Barretta is a house & trance DJ based in Jacksonville Beach, FL. He has held residencies at a rooftop cocktail bar and two lounges…". This is the best definitional block on the site and it's in the right place.
- **Venue pages**: real facts (address, venue type, "Eddie held a residency at SIP, soundtracking rooftop sunsets over the St. Johns River").
- **Blog posts**: dated (`datePublished` + `<time>`), 4 cited sources in a "Further reading" block plus `citation` in schema, and a local angle at the end ("Reading a room at Lucky's on a Friday still comes down to watching bodies, not scripts").
- **Tracklists**: the most original data on the site. Nobody else publishes them.

**Weak passages:**

- **Homepage About** ("With an ear for infectious beats and a heart that beats in sync with the dancefloor…"): four sentences with no quotable fact (no year, number, named residency or credential). This is the text above the fold.
- **Mix pages**: 351 words, mostly the tracklist. The intro is SoundCloud copy ("LUMINA ✨").
- **Wrong track counts on two mix pages.** `lib/mixes-store.ts:65-94` treats everything after the first paragraph as tracks when a description has no "…the following tracks:" lead-in. The two newest descriptions use that new format:
  - **Lumina**: page says **21 tracks**, its own copy says "an 18-track journey". Tracks 1–3 are prose ("Turn it up. Follow the light.").
  - **Aura**: track 1 is "Designed to elevate the mind and move the body…".
  - The other 54 tracklists checked clean. The error shows on the page, in `llms-full.txt` and in the homepage "Latest mix" card, so AI systems repeat it.
- **Shows times**: "Fri 14 Aug … 9:00 - 1:00 PM" should be 1:00 AM. That's a small error, but a machine reads it literally.
- **Blog posts mostly re-report trade press** (DJ LIFE, Soundstock, Magnetic). An engine will usually cite the original source, not a recap. Eddie's own take is in the last paragraph.

## 8. Top 5 highest-impact changes

1. **Fix the genre and bio at the source: SoundCloud.** *(Ben, off-site, ~10 min, biggest lever.)* Rewrite the SoundCloud profile and podcast description to lead with "house & trance DJ in Jacksonville Beach, FL", matching the site. Within a day or so it flows into `/podcast.rss`, Apple, Amazon, iHeart and Podder. Then update GigSalad and cueup.io to match, including past vs. current residencies. Every AI engine currently learns "tech house / melodic techno" from those profiles.
2. **One contact identity.** Set up a booking alias on the domain and use it for SoundCloud's podcast owner email, which also feeds `<itunes:email>`. Then align the llms.txt sentence about there being no public email. This removes the "email belongs to someone else" signal and takes the personal Gmail address out of public feeds.
3. **Retire (or re-sync) the stale Geordy pack.** Remove the `<link rel="alternate">` set in `app/layout.tsx:43-57` and the Dataset node in `app/page.tsx:159-200`, or get Geordy updating weekly as its own schema claims. A three-month-old duplicate of the site, advertised to AI crawlers in every page head, is worse than none.
4. **Put a factual answer block above the fold on the homepage** (draft in §10). Name, city, genres, named residencies with years, the Transcend podcast and its episode count, all in the first ~60 words, replacing or preceding the adjective-led About copy.
5. **Fix the tracklist parser's no-lead-in path** (`lib/mixes-store.ts:65-94`) so prose paragraphs aren't numbered as tracks. Either detect track-shaped lines, or treat the last prose run as intro. Then re-check Lumina (18) and Aura. Meanwhile, adding "…the following tracks:" to those two SoundCloud descriptions fixes them with no code change.

Next tier:

- **Venue pages**: add residency dates ("Resident DJ, 2025–2026") and a one-line "Who DJs at Lucky's?" answer. Venue pages draw most of the site's search impressions (Lucky's 910, Living Room Lounge 391, SIP 322, Dua 240 in 90 days) at ~0–1% CTR, because searchers want the venue itself. A clear Eddie-at-venue answer is what an AI engine can lift for "who plays house music at SIP Jacksonville."
- **Blog, per Google's Who/How/Why test**: add an author page or bio, and give the posts a one-line process note. They are drafted by the monthly blog agent but read as first-person Eddie ("I'll try ShoutOut on a request-heavy night"). Google asks for disclosure where readers would reasonably ask how content was made. Also lead each post with the local, first-hand angle, not the trade-press recap.
- **Original data from the tracklists** (the most citable thing the site could publish): 56 tracklists totalling ~1,320 entries → "most-played artists in Transcend", how the genre mix shifted across 2025–2026, what a bar set vs. a lounge set looks like. Nobody else has this data.
- **YouTube**: title Shorts with genre + venue + city ("Melodic house at SIP rooftop, Jacksonville") and put the site URL in descriptions. YouTube mentions correlate most strongly with AI visibility in the Ahrefs brand study (2025-12-12).
- **Reddit**: genuine participation in r/jacksonville / r/jaxbeach nightlife threads, not self-promotion. Perplexity draws heavily on Reddit.

## 9. Schema recommendations

None of these produces a Google rich result. They help machines resolve one entity with one set of facts.

1. **Link the blog author to the main entity.** `BlogPosting.author` is currently a fresh `Person` named "Eddie Barretta" with no `@id`, so to a parser it's a second, unrelated Eddie. Use `"author": {"@id": "https://eddiebarretta.com/#eddie"}` (same for `publisher`).
2. **Model Transcend as a podcast.** Mix pages say `inAlbum: MusicAlbum "Transcend"`, but it's a podcast. Add one `PodcastSeries` node (name, `webFeed: /podcast.rss`, `sameAs` Apple / Amazon / iHeart / YouTube playlist, `author` → `#eddie`). On mix pages, either keep `MusicRecording` and add `description`, or use `PodcastEpisode` with `partOfSeries`.
3. **Expand `sameAs`** on the MusicGroup node to the podcast listings (Apple, Amazon, iHeart) and GigSalad once its bio is fixed. Only add profiles whose facts match the site.
4. **Fix or remove the Dataset node** (stale `dateModified`, false `updateFrequency`), see §4.
5. **Venue pages**: keep `Place`, and add the relationship. A `MusicGroup` reference with a residency role, or past `Event` nodes with `startDate`/`endDate`, lets the "Eddie ↔ venue ↔ years" fact be machine-readable.
6. **Upcoming shows**: when the calendar has public dates, emit `Event` (`MusicEvent`) with `performer` → `#eddie`, `location` → the venue `Place`, and `startDate`. That one *is* Google rich-result eligible.
7. **FAQPage**: keep it. Google limits FAQ rich results to authoritative government and health sites, so expect no visual result, but it's harmless and machine-readable.

Keep the existing poetic lines after it, since they do say something about the vibe.

**Venue page (e.g. `/venues/luckys-bar`)**, add one answer line near the top:

> **Who DJs at Lucky's?** House and trance DJ Eddie Barretta has played guest sets at Lucky's in Jacksonville Beach [since/in year]; a recording of [set] is below.

**Mix page intro**, under the H1, a generated, factual line ahead of the SoundCloud copy:

> Lumina is an 18-track, 1:15:56 house and trance mix by Eddie Barretta, released September 27, 2026 as an episode of the Transcend podcast. It features Dombresky, YOTTO, Cassian, Sasha, BT and Gareth Emery.

(Easy to template from data that's already there: count, duration, date, top artists.)

**Blog posts**, move the local paragraph up. In "AI Just Moved From the Studio Into the Live DJ Set," the most original passage ("For a bar-circuit DJ, none of this changes the actual job. Reading a room at Lucky's on a Friday…") is the last paragraph. Open with it in two sentences, then the industry news. Consider question-style H2s where they fit naturally ("Will AI tools change what a bar DJ does?").

**Shows**: fix the after-midnight end time ("9:00 PM – 1:00 AM").
