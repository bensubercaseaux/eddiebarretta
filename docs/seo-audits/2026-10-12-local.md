## The headline finding from Search Console

Searches for hiring a DJ barely show this site at all. Nearly every impression comes from people looking up the **bars themselves**.

- **Last 90 days (07-12 → 10-10):** the top queries are venue lookups: "living room lounge" (233 impressions, position 7.7), "sip cocktail bar" (53), "dua lounge" plus variants (~90), "lemon bar …" (~90 across variants), "cataluna lounge" (18). They earn impressions but almost no clicks. These people want the bar's hours, not a DJ. The 2026-09-15 workspace review (`docs/search-console-review-2026-09-15.md`) found the same thing, and the venue titles have since gained "DJ at …".
- **DJ-intent queries, last 6 months:** a regex for `dj|eddie|barretta|house|trance|book|hire|party|event` returns **5 queries, 6 impressions, 0 clicks** ("beach dj" at 24.5, "high energy dj", "sip dj", "dj barrett", "barretta").
- **Service pages:** `/bar-lounge-dj` had 29 impressions at position 8.1 (1 click). `/private-event-dj` had **4 impressions at position 21**.
- GSC hides rare queries for privacy, so some branded searches are probably folded into the homepage's 100 impressions and 9 clicks. Even so, the picture is clear: **for "DJ in Jacksonville [Beach]" searches, the site isn't in the running yet.** For a local service, those searches are won mostly in the map pack, which needs a GBP.

Discrepancies to fix:
1. **City:** SoundCloud says Atlantic Beach; everything else says Jacksonville Beach. Pick one; the site's Jacksonville Beach is the obvious choice.
2. **What he plays:** four sources give four descriptions. Search engines and AI assistants build one picture of the entity from all of them. Write one sentence (e.g. "House and trance DJ in Jacksonville Beach; house-only for private parties") and use it on every profile.
3. **Contact:** the booking email is public on SoundCloud but not on the site. Either publish it on the site or take it off SoundCloud. The current split looks like two different businesses.

## 5. Local schema status

**Present and valid:** the homepage `@graph` has `MusicGroup` (`#eddie`, `location` → Jacksonville Beach, `makesOffer` → DJ booking Service, `sameAs`), `WebSite`, `FAQPage` and `SiteNavigationElement`. The service pages have `Service` (+ `provider` → `#eddie`) and `BreadcrumbList`. Venue pages have `Place` with full street addresses. Mix pages have `MusicRecording` + `AudioObject`. Event schema exists but shows **0** items because the calendar has no upcoming dates.

**Gaps:**
- `areaServed` is one free-text string ("Jacksonville, Jacksonville Beach & Northeast Florida") on MusicGroup and on both Services. Named places are clearer to parsers.
- `sameAs` includes `https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=…`, which is a search URL, not a profile. It also leaves out real profiles: GigSalad, Apple Podcasts.
- No `email` / `contactPoint`.
- No LocalBusiness-family type. Optional: a second type (below) gives `areaServed` and `priceRange` a proper home. It is **not** a ranking factor.

## 6. Location page quality
